Webb County Elections lists the November 3, 2026 Joint General Election and these deadlines: October 5 voter-registration deadline; October 19 early voting begins; October 23 mail-ballot application receipt deadline; October 30 early voting ends; November 3 Election Day and mail-ballot receipt deadline. Source: `https://www.webbcountytx.gov/291/Elections-Department`.

Early voting locations are populated two days before early voting; Election Day locations are populated two days before Election Day. Election Day voting hours are 7:00 a.m.–7:00 p.m. Source: `https://www.votetexas.gov/voting/where.html`. Portal: `https://goelect.txelections.civixapps.com/ivis-mvp-ui/#/login`.

## Campaign-finance source

The City Secretary's Campaign Finance Reports page states that Title 15 of the Texas Election Code requires candidates, officeholders, and specific-purpose committees to file reports. It also states that the City Secretary does not supervise, direct, or advise candidates regarding report content. Source: `https://www.cityoflaredo.com/departments/city-secretary-s-office/campaign-finance-reports`.

The page has sections for July 15, 2026 and January 15, 2026 semiannual reports for current officeholders, including several candidates in the November municipal election. Candidate-level research must distinguish campaign-finance reports from treasurer appointments and ballot applications, preserve report periods, and never treat missing documents as zero.
