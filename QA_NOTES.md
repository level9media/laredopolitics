# Laredo Politics Hub — Final QA

**Verified:** September 9, 2026

The production build completed successfully with no TypeScript errors. The XML sitemap contains **32 indexable routes**, and every route returned the application shell successfully in an automated smoke test.

## Desktop verification

The five-candidate hub renders all official candidates in a balanced single row at 1440px. Jorge A. Garza appears with a transparent limited-information notice rather than inferred biography or positions. Candidate cards, sponsor inventory, newsletter capture, and the Level Nine Media credit remain visually consistent.

The Water Infrastructure and Property Taxes pages render the complete long-form template: why the issue matters, current verified situation, key metrics, Lo Bueno/Lo Malo analysis, persistent upvote/downvote controls, voter questions, documented candidate positions, source list, sidebar advertising, correction contact, newsletter, and footer. Long pages remain readable with clear hierarchy and no horizontal overflow.

## Functional verification

Candidate, issue, voting, comparison, election, finance, fact-check, calendar, methodology, advertiser, and homepage routes compile and load. Dynamic canonical tags, descriptions, keywords, Open Graph metadata, and JSON-LD are generated by route. The public sitemap and robots file point to the deployed Manus domain. Vote selections persist in browser storage. Advertising and newsletter forms provide working prototype submission flows; production aggregation and CRM delivery still require a backend or connector.

## Mobile verification

Full-page checks at **390 × 844** confirmed clean stacking for the five-candidate hub, the long-form Water Infrastructure article, and the voting-resource hub. Mobile navigation remains accessible, card text stays readable, issue metrics collapse to one column, voting controls remain within the viewport, source links retain adequate tap area, and sponsor units preserve clear paid-placement labeling. No horizontal overflow or clipped headings were observed.

## Browser interaction check

The live Water Infrastructure route loaded with the expected canonical title, complete article content, both vote controls, official-source links, sponsor CTA, newsletter, and footer navigation. The first automated click attempt used a case-sensitive text selector and did not find the visually present “Útil” control; the test selector will be corrected before final delivery.

The corrected browser test passed. Selecting **Útil** updated `aria-pressed` to `true` and persisted `up` under the issue-specific local-storage key. The live route also exposed the correct production canonical URL, a non-empty meta description, Article JSON-LD, and an advertiser-form link targeting `/#advertise-form`.

The advertiser deep-link test also passed. Opening `/#advertise-form` landed with the form in the viewport. The form contains five required conversion fields—name, business, email, budget, and message—plus an optional phone field and the expected **Solicitar información** submit action.

## English-first, candidate portraits, and advertising carousel

Full-page mobile checks at 390 × 844 confirmed clean single-column stacking, readable English and Spanish typography, intact candidate portrait crops, visible carousel controls, and no horizontal overflow on the homepage, candidate hub, or Spanish Water Infrastructure guide.

The primary homepage opened in English with the correct English title, an English `html lang` value, the production canonical URL, and English, Spanish, and `x-default` alternate links. Four verified candidate portraits rendered in the homepage candidate section, while the fifth candidate retained the intended initials fallback. Final validation passed with no TypeScript errors. The production build succeeds, all **64 sitemap routes** return the application shell, and all four optimized candidate portrait assets exist.
